How the photos are made

  • Rendered from the CAD files on a server, the way iJewel's batch renderer works: every metal, shape and colour, from every angle.
  • Diamonds are traced through their real facets, reflecting inside them as real stones do, with fire; metal is lit like a jeweller's light tent and uses the CAD's exact surface curves.
  • The 360° shows the ring lying down, turning under fixed lights like a turntable shoot, with a touch of macro-lens depth of field.
  • Shapes other than the head's own are fitted to its seat, to show the stone; a production head per shape would place prongs to match.

Why it loads fast

  • Photos are ready-made files (WebP, two sizes): a phone downloads the half-size one.
  • The 360° video (H.264 MP4, starts streaming at once) loads only when opened.
  • 3D loads only on request. Choices without photos yet are shown in 3D until they are rendered.
